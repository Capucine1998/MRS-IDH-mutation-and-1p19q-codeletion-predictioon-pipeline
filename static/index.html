Research prototype

Magnetic Resonance Spectroscopy tool for MEGA‑PRESS processing and molecular status prediction for gliomas

Automatic MRS preprocessing and diagnostic support (currently adapted for gliomas), designed to democratize spectroscopy analysis for broad research and molecular characterization in complex neuropathologies.

Not for clinical use. Research / education only.

At a glance

  • MEGA‑PRESS preprocessing with quality checks
  • Reports saved as HTML + text-only PNG
  • Classifier for IDH & 1p/19q (from LCModel outputs)

Quick start

  1. Open Processing and upload your anonymized MEGA-PRESS .dcm files.
  2. Get your quantified processed spectra from LCModel.
  3. Run Classifier using LCModel outputs.
