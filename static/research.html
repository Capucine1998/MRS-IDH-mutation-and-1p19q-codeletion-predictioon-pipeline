Research

This project stems from a PhD research program aimed at identifying glioma-specific metabolic biomarkers using Magnetic Resonance Spectroscopy (MRS) to enhance non-invasive tumor classification.

Publication

Multi-center ensemble classifier for IDH mutation and 1p/19q codeletion prediction in gliomas using MR spectroscopy.
